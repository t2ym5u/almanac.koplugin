# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-04

### Changed
- Uses less memory: the tool loads when you open it, not when KOReader
  starts, so it no longer takes memory or startup time while unused.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added
- Month view: Monday first (Sunday for the US / English Canada), ISO week
  numbers, today in black, public holidays on grey, principal moon phases.
  Swipe or arrows to change month, tap the month name to jump to any date or
  year.
- Day panel: weekday, day of the year, ISO week, distance from today,
  holidays, moon phase (time of the principal phase, or illumination and age).
- Public holidays for France (with an Alsace-Moselle option), the United
  Kingdom (England & Wales, substitute days and one-off holidays), the United
  States (federal, observed days), Germany, Spain, Belgium, Switzerland and
  Canada; the default follows the UI language.
- Date calculator: days between two dates (with or without the end day,
  working days without weekends and holidays), date ± days / weeks / months /
  years, weekday of a date, age from a birth date with the next birthday.
